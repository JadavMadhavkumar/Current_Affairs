### National News and Programs

- **Ramsar Sites:**  
  - Two new Ramsar sites designated in Bihar: Gokul Wetland (Buxar) and Udaipur Lake (West Champaran).  
  - India now has 93 Ramsar sites, highest in Asia.

- **Prime Minister Matsya Sampada Yojana:**  
  - Completed 5 years as of 10 Sept 2025.  
  - Aimed at boosting fisheries sector with financial support, insurance, and market access.

- **My Bharat Initiative:**  
  - Autonomous body under Ministry of Commerce & Industry.  
  - Supports 100 agri-food startups to enhance exports, targeting $50 billion agri-food exports by 2030.

- **Vikram Processor:**  
  - India’s first indigenous 32-bit space-grade microprocessor launched on 2 Sept 2025.  
  - Developed by ISRO’s Semiconductor Lab, fabrication and packaging in Mohali, Punjab.  
  - Used for space applications, can withstand extreme temperatures.

- **Umeed Portal:**  
  - Centralized digital platform for registering and regulating Waqf properties across India.  
  - Launched by Kiren Rijiju and Ministry of Minority Affairs.

- **Blue Ports Initiative:**  
  - Collaboration between India and FAO to develop world-class sustainable fisheries ports.  
  - Three pilot sites: Vanakbara Dug (Gujarat), Karaikal (Puducherry), and Jakhau (Gujarat).

- **Other National Initiatives:**  
  - NCERT celebrated 65th foundation day (1 Sept).  
  - Make in India initiative completed 11 years (25 Sept).  
  - Namaste Bharat train became fastest train in India (160 km/hr on Delhi–Meerut stretch).  
  - AI-powered interactive mobile application launched for India Mobile Congress 2025.

- **Defense and Science:**  
  - NISAR Satellite (ISRO-NASA joint mission) transmitted first radar images.  
  - Jupiter: Europe’s first exascale supercomputer developed by Germany.  
  - Pratyush: Indian space-based radio telescope to detect cosmic dawn signals.  
  - Agni Prime: India’s next-generation nuclear-capable intermediate-range ballistic missile successfully test-fired from rail-based mobile launcher.  
  - India’s first Dugong Conservation Reserve recognized by IUCN located in Gulf of Mannar and Palk Bay, Tamil Nadu.  
  - India’s first vulture conservation portal “Vulture Network” launched in Assam to monitor endangered vulture species.
