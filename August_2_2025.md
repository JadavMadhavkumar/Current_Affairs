### Key Highlights and Insights

#### 1. **Indices and Reports**
- **Global AI City Index 2025**  
  - Released by **Counterpoint Research**.  
  - Evaluates cities on AI capacity, investment, and innovation.  
  - **Singapore** ranks 1st globally.  
  - **Bengaluru** is India’s top city (#26 globally), followed by Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, and Kolkata.  
  - India lacks a regulatory framework for AI governance.  
- **India Electric Mobility Index 2024**  
  - Released by **NITI Aayog**.  
  - Top states/UTs for EV readiness: **Delhi, Maharashtra, Chandigarh**.  
  - **Karnataka** has the highest number of EV charging stations.  
  - Total EVs on Indian roads exceeded 5 million, with over 25,000 public charging stations.  
- **District Flood Severity Index**  
  - Developed by researchers from **IIT Delhi and IIT Gandhinagar**.  
  - Measures flood severity based on flood duration, affected area, deaths, injuries, and population affected.  
  - **Patna** ranks highest in flood severity, while **Thiruvananthapuram** experiences frequent flood events but not severe floods.  
- **National Annual Report and Index on Women Safety 2025**  
  - Released by **National Commission for Women (NCW)**, not NITI Aayog.  
  - Surveyed 12,000 women across 31 cities.  
  - **Kohima** ranked safest for women (82.9% safety score), while cities like Ranchi, Srinagar, Kolkata, Delhi rank low.  
- **State Health Regulatory Excellence Index**  
  - Launched by the **Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare** under the leadership of **J.P. Nadda**.  
  - Evaluates states on drug manufacturing and distribution regulatory systems.  
- **Smart City Index 2025**  
  - Released by **International Institute of Management Development (IMD)**.  
  - Top global smart cities: **Zurich (Switzerland), Oslo (Norway), Geneva, Dubai, Abu Dhabi**.  
  - No Indian city in the top 100; **Delhi** is the highest-ranked Indian city, followed by Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru.  
  - Smart city criteria: health and safety, mobility, opportunities, governance.

#### 2. **Defense Updates**
- **Operation Alert A:** BSF security operation along the Indo-Pak border in Rajasthan (11-17 August) to prevent infiltration.  
- **Operation Akal:** Joint anti-terror operation in Akal Kulshan forest, Jammu & Kashmir, neutralizing militants.  
- **Agni-5 Missile:**  
  - India successfully test-fired the **Agni-5**, an intercontinental ballistic missile (ICBM) with a range over 5000 km.  
  - Capable of carrying up to 3 nuclear warheads simultaneously (MIRV technology).  
  - Developed by **DRDO**, launched from Integrated Test Range, Odisha.  
- **Military Exercises:**  
  - **Exercise Maitri:** 14th joint bilateral military exercise between India and Thailand in Meghalaya, Sep 2025.  
  - Other exercises include **Garud Shakti** (India-Indonesia-Maldives), **Uchi Freedom Shield** (US-South Korea), **Bright Star** (Egypt-US).  
- **CISF's First All-Women Commando Unit:** Launched in Madhya Pradesh with 30 women trainees.  
- **INS Udaygiri and INS Himagiri:**  
  - INS Udaygiri built by Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited (MDL).  
  - INS Himagiri built by Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers (GRSE).  
  - Both are advanced stealth frigates commissioned under Project 17A.

#### 3. **Science, Technology, and Environment**
- **Hope Station in Ladakh:**  
  - Developed by Bangalore-based **Protoplanet** in collaboration with **ISRO** at 14,500 ft altitude in Socar, Ladakh.  
  - Simulates Moon and Mars conditions for human outer planet exploration research.  
- **Leap One Satellite Mission:**  
  - First commercial satellite launched by Hyderabad-based **Dhruva Space** using SpaceX Falcon 9.  
  - Focus on AI functions and real-time data analysis in Low Earth Orbit (LEO).  
- **Indian Space Station:**  
  - Planned for Low Earth Orbit (~450 km).  
  - To be completed by 2035 with 5 modules; first module ready by 2028.  
- **NASA SOOR AI Model:**  
  - Developed in collaboration with IBM to predict space weather, solar flares, and their effects on Earth's atmosphere.  
- **International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA):**  
  - Headquartered in New Delhi.  
  - Nepal recently joined as a member.  
  - Protects seven big cat species including tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar, and puma.
