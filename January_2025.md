- **Padma Awards 2025:**  
  - Padma Vibhushan: 7 recipients including Kumudini Lakhia (Kathak dancer) and Rajnikanth (actor).

  - Best Actor: Brendan Fraser for *The Whale*  
- **Lifetime Achievement Awards:** Jaipur International Film Festival honored late Yash Chopra and Shyam Benegal.

#### 4. **Government Initiatives and Schemes**
- **National Turmeric Board:** Headquarters in Nizamabad, Telangana; Palya Ganga Reddy appointed first Chairperson.  
- **Road Accident Victim Cashless Treatment Scheme:** Maximum cashless treatment coverage up to ₹1.5 lakh under Ministry of Road Transport and Highways.  
- **Indian Aircraft Act 2024:** Replaces Aircraft Act 1934 from 1 January 2025; regulates air traffic management but not consumer protection.  
- **Bharat Poll Portal:** Launched by Ministry of Home Affairs for real-time sharing of data with international law enforcement.  
- **New Year Government Calendar 2025:** Published in 13 Indian languages by Ministry of Information and Broadcasting.  
- **National Internet Exchange of India (NIXI):** Launched Internet governance internship and capacity-building scheme.  
- **Rythu Bharosa Scheme (Telangana):** ₹12,000 per acre annually for farmers on agricultural lands.

#### 5. **National News and Developments**
- **GI Tagged Products:**  
  - Kanjivaram Silk - Tamil Nadu  
  - Kalamkari Painting on Bamboo - Andhra Pradesh  
  - Pochampalli Ikat - Telangana  
  - Ganjifa Art - Karnataka  
  - Etikoppaka Wooden Toys - Andhra Pradesh  
- **Anji Khad Bridge:** India’s first cable-stayed railway bridge located in Reasi district, Jammu & Kashmir; second highest railway bridge in India after Chenab Bridge.  
- **Uniform Civil Code (UCC) in Uttarakhand:**  
  - Legal marriage age: 21 (men), 18 (women)  
  - Ban on polygamy, equal inheritance rights, mandatory registration of marriages, divorces, and live-in relationships.  
- **Organic Fisheries Cluster:** Launched in Sikkim under PM Matsya Sampada Yojana, promoting organic fish farming.

#### 6. **International Affairs**
- **BRICS Expansion:** Brazil announced Indonesia as a new full member, making BRICS a 10-country bloc.  
- **US Passport Index 2025:** India ranked 85th, slipping 5 places; Singapore and Japan top the list.  
- **Global Future Skills Index 2025:** India ranked 25th overall; second in "Future of Work" category after the USA.  
- **World Economic Forum Global Risks Report 2025:** Released highlighting economic uncertainty and risks globally.

#### 7. **Science and Technology**
- **Wetland Cities Accreditation:** Udaipur (Rajasthan) and Indore (MP) are first two Indian cities accredited as International Wetland Cities.  
- **Blue Flag Certification:** Kerala’s Kovalam and Chal Beach received certification for cleanliness and environmental management from Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE).  
- **Human Metapneumovirus (HMPV):** Respiratory virus causing cold-like symptoms; first case in India detected in Bangalore; no vaccine available yet.  
- **ISRO 100th Launch:** Successfully launched NVS-02 satellite on GSLV F15; total 548 satellites launched by ISRO so far.  
- **Microgravity Crop Experiment:** ISRO’s PSLV C60 mission carried out successful germination of cowpea seeds under microgravity conditions for about 4 days.  
- **Space Docking Experiment (Spadix Mission):** India became the 4th country to dock two separate satellites in space on 16 Jan 2025.  
- **Mission Mausam:** Spearheaded by Indian Meteorological Department, IIT Mumbai, and National Centre for Medium Range Weather Forecasting with a budget of ₹2000 crore focused on improved weather forecasting and climate resilience.  
- **DRDO Developments:**  
  - *Him Kavach*: Multi-layered clothing system providing protection in -20°C to -60°C, intended for Himalayan troops.  
  - *Trojan 155 mm Artillery Gun:* Developed jointly with France; export order from Armenia.  
  - *Nag Missile Mk2:* Indigenous third-generation anti-tank guided missile with 20% indigenous content.  
- **Euro Drone:** Multi-national project with Germany, France, Italy, and Spain; designed for air surveillance, anti-submarine warfare, and target acquisition missions.

#### 8. **Defense Updates**
- **Exercise Varuna:** Indo-French naval exercise ongoing since 2001; 2024 edition held in Mediterranean Sea.  
- **Fast Patrol Vessels:** *Amulya* and *Akshay* launched by Goa Shipyard Limited with 60% indigenous content for Indian Coast Guard.  
- **Scorpene-class Submarine (Project 75):** Sixth submarine *Vagshar* delivered by Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders.  
- **Border Roads Organization:** Developed Z-Mor tunnel (8650 ft elevation) connecting Srinagar to Sonmarg, not directly linking to Ladakh.  
- **Indian Navy:** Participated in multinational exercises in Indian Ocean and Pacific region.  
- **Upcoming Technologies:** Nag Mk2 missile inducted; Euro Drone under development with multi-nation collaboration.
