- **National Maritime Varun Award:** Rajesh Unni received India’s highest individual maritime sector recognition.

- **Raman Nair:** Lifetime Achievement Award in Basketball (Arjuna Awardee).

### International Affairs

- **World Economic Forum (WEF):** Founder Klaus Schwab resigned after 50 years; interim chairperson Peter Brabeck-Letmathe appointed.  
- **Huvajiang Grand Canyon Bridge (China):** Will be the world's highest suspension bridge at 625 meters above river level, opening expected June 2025.  
- **Basabi Festival:** Celebrated by the Chakma, Marma, and Tripura communities in Bangladesh's Chittagong Hill Tracts.  
- **International Solar Alliance (ISA):** Mauritius became the first African country to sign the Country Partnership Framework; Kenya is not the first.  
- **BIMSTEC:** 6th summit held in Bangkok, Thailand; Bangladesh assumed chairmanship for 2025-26.  
- **Ecuador:** Daniel Noboa re-elected president for a full term.  
- **Commonwealth Secretary-General:** Charlie Brocha became the first African woman in the role.

### Science, Technology & Environment

- **Space Missions:**  
  - ISRO sending **tardigrades (water bears)** to space in the ExoMars mission to study survival in microgravity.  
  - NASA's Curiosity rover found evidence of warm, wet climate on Mars in the past.  
  - SpaceX’s **“From To” mission** was the first human spaceflight over Earth’s polar regions.  
  - IISc Bengaluru developed **Angstrom-scale semiconductor chips**, significantly smaller and energy-efficient.  
- **Environmental Issues:**  
  - River blindness (Onchocerciasis) transmitted by black flies studied by Indian Council of Medical Research.  
  - DPS Wetland in Maharashtra declared a flamingo conservation reserve, connected to Thane Creek Ramsar site.  
- **Water Management:**  
  - **Modernization of Command Area Development and Water Management Scheme** approved with ₹1600 crore initial outlay for 2025-26.  
- **Rare Species:**  
  - Discovery of rare golden tabby tiger at Kaziranga National Park, Assam.  
  - Discovery of rare long-snouted snake in Dudhwa Tiger Reserve, Uttar Pradesh.  
- **Indigenous AI Development:**  
  - Bengaluru-based startup "Colossal Biosciences" genetically engineered dire wolf pups.  
- **Agricultural Developments:**  
  - ICAR and IIISR developed new varieties of turmeric and sweet potato to enhance food security in tribal areas.

### Defense Updates

- **Exercises:**  
  - DUSTLIK-6: India-Uzbekistan joint exercise, annual, alternating locations (Pune in 2025).  
  - Desert Flag 10: Multinational air combat exercise in UAE including India (April-May 2025).  
  - EXERCISE AKRAMAN: Indian Air Force drill with Rafale and Sukhoi-30 aircraft after Pulwama incident.  
  - Africa-India Multilateral Maritime Exercise (EAMY) 2025 held in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.  
- **Indigenous Developments:**  
  - IASV Surat: India’s first AI-enabled warship, tested medium-range surface-to-air missile.  
  - DRDO developed MK2A laser-directed energy weapon system.  
  - Commissioning of first dedicated nuclear submarine base "IAS Varsha" in Andhra Pradesh (2026).  
- **Security:**  
  - 11th Cobra Battalion of CRPF to be established in Jammu & Kashmir for counter-terror and anti-Naxal operations.

  - SBI launched **Tata New SBI Credit Card** in collaboration with Tata Digital.  
  - Bank of Maharashtra became first bank to install ATM in a train (BOMBAY STOCK EXCHANGE train).  
- **Corporate:**

- **Health Insurance:**

  - Kerala declared first Indian state to achieve total digital literacy including elderly population.
