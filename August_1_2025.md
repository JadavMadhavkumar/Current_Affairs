#### 4. **Constitutional and Legislative Updates**
- **13th Constitutional Amendment Bill 2025:**
  - Proposes **automatic removal** of ministers (PM, CM, others) detained for 30+ consecutive days on serious charges (punishable by 5+ years).
  - Currently under joint parliamentary committee review due to concerns about misuse.
- **Article 370:**
  - Abrogated in 2019; special status for Jammu & Kashmir ended.
  - Drafted by N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar in 1949.
- **Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Bill 2025:**
  - Bans online real money gaming (skill or chance-based).
  - Penalties include imprisonment up to 3 years and fines up to ₹1 crore.
  - Promotes e-sports and social gaming.
- **Indian Ports Bill 2025:**
  - Replaces Indian Ports Act 1908.
  - Aims to modernize ports, simplify documentation, encourage PPP and FDI.
- **Uttarakhand Freedom of Religion and Anti-Conversion Amendment Bill 2025:**
  - Increases jail sentences for forced religious conversions (3 years to life imprisonment).
  - Introduced a Minority Education Board for affiliation of minority institutions.
- **Sikkim Sabbatical Leave Scheme:**
  - First state to introduce sabbatical leave for government employees with 50% pay for up to 1880 days.
