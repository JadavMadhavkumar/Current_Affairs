#### 4. **National News & Initiatives**
- **Vibrant Villages Programme Phase II:** Approved by Cabinet for border areas development (Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Gujarat, J&K, Ladakh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland). Focus on infrastructure, education, tourism, self-help groups, sustainable livelihoods.
- **Policy & Governance:**
  - 10th Governing Council Meeting of NITI Aayog chaired by PM Modi; theme: **“Developed States for Developed India @2047.”**
  - **Operation Sindoor:** Indian Armed Forces operation targeting terrorist infrastructure in Pakistan and PoK.
- **E-Passport Initiative:** India to roll out chip-based e-passports in collaboration with India Security Press, Nashik, from May 2025.
- **Revised SHAKTI Scheme:** Coal allocation to power plants through transparent auctions; two windows - fixed notified price and premium price auction.
- **India’s Updated Coastline:** Revised length is **7,198.81 km** (previously ~7,561.60 km) due to improved measurement techniques using geospatial technology.
- **Census Updates:** India conducts census every 10 years (not 15); caste data approval received for the 2027 census.
- **India's First Certified Green Municipal Bond:** Issued by Ghaziabad Municipal Corporation to fund tertiary sewage treatment plant; ₹150 crore raised.

#### 6. **International News**
- **UN Charter Article 51:** Recognizes the right of countries to self-defense if attacked. India invoked this in response to Pakistan’s claims post Operation Sindoor.
- **Diego Garcia:** Largest island of Chagos Archipelago; US military base leased from UK; UK agreed to return other islands to Mauritius but base remains.
- **New BRICS Members:** Algeria recently joined the New Development Bank (NDB); headquarters in Shanghai, China (not Beijing).
- **Interpol:** Issues 9 color-coded notices for international policing; India issued its first Silver Notice for illicit assets related to Shubham Shaukeen case.
- **US & Israel:** US developing “Golden Dome” missile defense system; Israel developed “Harop” loitering missile used by Indian forces.
- **Vatican:** Robert Prevost appointed new Pope.
- **Australia:** Anthony Albanese re-elected PM.
- **Germany:** Olaf Scholz succeeded by Friedrich Merz as Chancellor.

#### 7. **Science & Technology**
- **NASA’s Quantum Gravity Gradiometer Pathfinder:** Mission to measure Earth's gravity variations; applications in identifying underground water and geological features.
- **India’s First Genome-Edited Rice Varieties:** DRR Dhan 100 Kamla and Pusa DST Rices developed using CRISPR-Cas9 technology.
- **India’s First Genome-Edited Sheep:** Developed in Jammu & Kashmir (Sher-e-Kashmir University of Agricultural Sciences); gene edited: **Myostatin gene** to enhance muscle growth by 30%.
- **PSLV C61 Mission:** Intended to launch EOS-09 Radar Imaging Satellite; failed on 18th May 2025; mission to be retried.
- **India Forecasting System:** Developed by IITM Pune with 6 km resolution, world’s highest resolution weather forecast.
- **Asiatic Lion Census 2025:** 891 lions recorded, a 32% increase from last census (674 in 2020); mainly in Gir Forest, Gujarat.
- **Stratospheric Airship Trials:** India conducted flight trials reaching 17 km altitude for surveillance.

#### 8. **Defence**
- **Operation Sindoor:** Targeted 9 terrorist camps in Pakistan and PoK (Bahawalpur, Kotli, Muzaffarabad, etc.).
- **Exercise Nomadic Elephant 2025:** Joint Indian Army and Mongolian Armed Forces exercise.
- **S-400 Missile System:** Long-range surface-to-air missile system procured from Russia; known as "Sudarshan Chakra" in India.
- **BrahMos Missile:** Supersonic cruise missile jointly developed by India and Russia; nuclear-capable; used in Operation Sindoor.
- **IAS Tamal:** Advanced stealth frigate from Russia’s Krivak-III class; part of 4-ship deal between India and Russia.
- **IA SV Kadiniya:** Modern stealth warship inspired by ancient maritime designs; built using traditional materials.
- **Harop Loitering Munition:** Developed by Israel Aerospace Industries; used as a suicide drone.
- **Territorial Army:** Not part of regular army; can be mobilized under Rule 33 by Central Government to support army during emergencies.
- **Anti-Naxal Operations:** Operations “Black Forest” and “Mission Sankalp” conducted in Chhattisgarh-Telangana border.
- **New Defence PSU Mini Ratna Status:** Given to Mishra Dhatu Nigam Limited, Armed Vehicles Nigam Limited, and India Optel Limited.
- **Other Defence Developments:**  
  - Anti-submarine warfare shallow water craft "IAS Aranala" inducted.  
  - BrahMos Aerospace plant inaugurated in Lucknow.  
  - ITBP scaled Mount Makalu, world’s 5th highest peak.
