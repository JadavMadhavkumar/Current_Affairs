### Important National News and Schemes

- **Maratha Military Landscapes:** Declared UNESCO World Heritage Site; 12 forts mainly in Maharashtra plus one in Tamil Nadu (Gingee Fort); cultural category.  
- **Employment Linked Incentive (ELI) Scheme:** Approved to incentivize formal employment generation, especially in manufacturing; covers freshers and employers linked with EPFO; ₹99,446 crore outlay for 2025-27.  
- **PhenoM India National Biobank:** Launched by CSIR-IGIB, storing biological samples and health data for population-based research.  
- **National Overseas Scholarship:** For marginalized communities pursuing higher education abroad; eligibility includes age limit, income ceiling, and academic merit.  
- **TALASH Initiative:** Collaborative between National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS) and UNICEF India to assess and enhance tribal students' aptitude, life skills, and self-esteem.  
- **PM e-Drive:** Incentive scheme promoting electric trucks with scrappage of old diesel trucks; budget ₹500 crore allocated.  
- **PM DhanDhanya Agricultural Scheme:** Covering 100 districts with low agricultural productivity aiming to improve productivity and credit access; ₹24,000 crore outlay for six years.  
- **Prime Minister Professorship:** Launched by NRF to enhance research in state universities by retired eminent scientists; tenure 5 years with fellowship and research grant.  
- **Removal of Judges:** Requires minimum 100 Lok Sabha and 50 Rajya Sabha MPs to sign notice; process involves inquiry and 2/3 majority voting in Parliament.  
- **Bills of Lading 2025:** Replaces 1856 Act; modernizes maritime shipping laws to simplify legal and operational framework.  
- **ADITI Scheme:** Launched by Ministry of Power for promoting clean and energy-efficient technology in MSMEs with financial incentives.  
- **Global Conference on India’s Manuscript Heritage:** Organized by Ministry of Culture to preserve and promote India’s manuscript legacy.

- **Karnataka:** Reopened historic Karnak Bridge renamed Sindoor Bridge.

### International News and Developments

- **BRICS 17th Summit:** Held in Rio de Janeiro (Brazil); Indonesia admitted as member; focus on global governance reforms, peace, and sustainable development.  
- **Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD):** Africa’s largest hydroelectric project on Blue Nile near Sudan border; 4.46 GW capacity; regional water concerns remain.  
- **Disputed Preah Temple:** Between Thailand and Cambodia; historical conflict over border temples.  
- **WHO 3/35 Initiative:** To increase prices by 50% by 2035 on tobacco, alcohol, and sugary drinks to reduce consumption.  
- **Russia:** First country to recognize Taliban government in Afghanistan.  
- **Elon Musk:** Launched new political party “The America Party” with Indian-origin Vaibhav Taneja as CFO.  
- **NASA-ISRO NISAR Satellite:** Dual-band synthetic aperture radar launched for earth observation, natural disaster monitoring, and cryosphere studies.  
- **India’s Largest Black Hole Merger (GW23123):** Detected via LIGO, Virgo, and KAGRA detectors from 12 billion light-years away.  
- **Asteroid Terrestrial Impact Last Alert System (ATLAS):** Developed by University of Hawaii with NASA funding to detect near-earth objects.  
- **Quantum Baby:** Switzerland-approved malaria vaccine for infants under 6 months.  
- **India Study by IISc:** Comprehensive study on aging biomarkers to understand aging in Indian population.
