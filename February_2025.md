### National & International Highlights

- **Indian Coast Guard:** Established 1 Feb 1977 (assistance phase), formally inaugurated 19 Aug 1978 by Morarji Desai.
- **New Infrastructure:** India’s first indigenous automated biomedical waste treatment plant “Srijan” inaugurated at AIIMS New Delhi.
- **Land Survey Program (NAKSHA):** Under Ministry of Rural Development, digitizing land records in urban areas, started from Raisen, Madhya Pradesh.
- **Government eMarketplace (GeM):** Overseen by Ministry of Commerce & Industry, promoting women and youth entrepreneurs.
- **International Relations:** India assumed chairmanship of Bay of Bengal Inter-Governmental Organization (BOB-IGO) in 2025, taking over from Bangladesh.
- **Space Missions:** 
  - *Athena Lander Mission* by Intuitive Machines with NASA payloads for lunar south pole water ice detection.
  - *PUNCH Mission* by NASA for solar corona and solar wind imaging via satellite constellation.
  - *M-Four Mission* - India's private spaceflight to the International Space Station (ISS), with Group Captain Shubanshu Shukla as pilot.
  - Sunita Williams broke spacewalking record (62 hrs 6 mins).
- **Climate Risk Index 2025:** India ranks 6th most affected country due to extreme weather events over 30 years.
- **Corruption Perception Index 2023:** India ranked 96th out of 180 countries; Denmark least corrupt.
- **India’s Solar Milestone:** Achieved 100 GW installed solar power capacity, targeting 500 GW by 2030.
- **International Big Cat Alliance:** Launched under National Tiger Conservation Authority to protect seven big cat species; headquartered in India with 5 initial member countries.
